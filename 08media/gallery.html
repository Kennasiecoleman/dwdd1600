My Inspiration

My inspiration to create art comes from a lifelong desire to turn my emotions and observations into something tangible. But what truly deepened my passion were my experiences in art galleries—especially the Springville Museum of Art. Walking through those halls, surrounded by pieces that seemed to breathe with history, intention, and feeling, sparked a sense of possibility in me. Seeing how artists captured their own worlds made me want to explore and share mine.
